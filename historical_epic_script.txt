THE KINGDOM OF ANKOR - HISTORICAL WAR EPIC (DEMO SCRIPT)

INT. ROYAL THRONE ROOM - NIGHT

KING RUDRADEVA (50), wearing a GOLDEN CROWN and HEAVY SILK ROBES, sits on an carved STONE THRONE. Flame torches flicker along the high marble pillars.

GENERAL VARDHAN (40), clad in STEEL CHEST ARMOR and carrying a DOUBLE-EDGED SWORD, kneels before the throne. He places a SCROLL WITH ROYAL SEAL on the table.

RUDRADEVA
Is the army prepared for the siege?

VARDHAN
The siege engines are locked at the northern fortress gates, Your Highness.

EXT. FORTRESS WALLS - NIGHT

Rain lashes against giant REINFORCED WOODEN GATES. 

SOLDIERS in LEATHER ARMOR stand guard on the STONE PARAPETS. A massive WAR DRUM beats rhythmically in the background.

COMMANDER DEVI (30) wears a BRONZE HELMET and wields a LONG BOW. She draws a BURNING ARROW and aims into the darkness.

DEVI
(shouting)
Hold positions! Wait for the signal torch!

INT. WAR ROOM - NIGHT

A large TACTICAL WAR MAP carved in WOOD lies on a central TABLE. 

CHIEF COUNSELOR AMARYA places COPPER COINS and CARVED FIGURES on the map. A BRONZE LAMP illuminates the parchment maps.

VARDHAN enters holding a SPEAR and a LEATHER SHIELD.

VARDHAN
The vanguard has crossed the river. Prepare the cavalry!

EXT. BATTLEFIELD - DAWN

The sun rises over misty hills. HUNDREDS OF WARRIORS stand in formation.

A HYDRAULIC CATAPULT is loaded with a BURNING BOULDER. A WAR ELEPHANT outfitted with IRON TUSK GUARDS leads the front line.

VARDHAN raises his sword high in the air as the battle horns sound across the valley.
