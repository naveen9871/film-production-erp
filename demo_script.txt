MIDNIGHT RUN - SHORT SCREENPLAY (DEMO SCRIPT)

INT. POLICE STATION - INTERROGATION ROOM - NIGHT

OFFICER PRIYA SHARMA sits across a metal table from RAJAN MEHTA,
a nervous middle-aged man in a torn suit. A single overhead light
swings above them. A CCTV CAMERA records the session.

PRIYA wears a khaki uniform and carries a LEATHER NOTEBOOK. She
places a MANILA FOLDER on the table.

PRIYA
Where were you on the night of the 12th?

RAJAN
I was at home. I swear.

INT. RAJAN'S APARTMENT - LIVING ROOM - NIGHT (FLASHBACK)

A cramped apartment. RAJAN sits on a worn SOFA watching a small
TELEVISION. His MOBILE PHONE buzzes. He ignores it. A CEILING FAN
rotates slowly above. The walls are covered with newspaper clippings.

RAJAN picks up an OLD PHOTOGRAPH from the coffee table and stares
at it.

EXT. MARINE DRIVE - MUMBAI - NIGHT

PRIYA walks along the seafront. She wears plain clothes now - a
long TRENCH COAT. A POLICE JEEP follows slowly behind her.

She stops and makes a call on her SMARTPHONE. The city lights
reflect off the wet road.

PRIYA
(into phone)
He is lying. Get the warrant.

INT. COURT ROOM - DAY

A crowded courtroom. JUDGE KRISHNAMURTHY sits at the bench wearing
BLACK ROBES. ADVOCATE MEHTA stands at the podium with a BRIEFCASE
full of documents.

RAJAN sits in the WITNESS BOX. He wears a formal WHITE SHIRT.

The COURT STENOGRAPHER types rapidly. A large PROJECTOR SCREEN
shows CCTV FOOTAGE.

INT. POLICE STATION - EVIDENCE ROOM - NIGHT

PRIYA enters wearing LATEX GLOVES. She opens an EVIDENCE BOX and
removes a KNIFE wrapped in a PLASTIC BAG. She photographs it with
a DIGITAL CAMERA.

CONSTABLE RAJ assists her, holding a TORCH. He wears full POLICE
UNIFORM. A steel EVIDENCE SHELF runs along the entire wall.
